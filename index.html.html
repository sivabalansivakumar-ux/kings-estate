02
Account Audit — Critical Issues Found
Critical
Display Network Enabled Inside Search Campaigns
Your search campaigns are serving ads across Google's Display Network — websites, apps, YouTube, and Gmail — without your knowledge. Of the 50,547 total impressions recorded, only 1,782 came from actual search queries. This means 96.5% of impressions are display placements with poor conversion intent, diluting your budget, inflating your impression count, and masking true search performance. The overall CTR of 1.77% and CPA of £465.09 in the Sell With Us campaign are direct symptoms of this setting. Disabling it immediately will give cleaner data and more efficient budget allocation.
Critical — Causing Out-of-Area Leads
Location Targeting Set to "Presence or Interest"
This is the direct cause of your out-of-area lead problem. The "Presence or Interest" setting tells Google to show your ads to anyone who has recently searched about or shown interest in Tunbridge Wells — even if they are physically located in London, Birmingham, or anywhere else. Switching to "Presence Only" restricts your ads to users who are physically located within your target geography, which is essential for a locally-focused estate agency. This single change will immediately reduce irrelevant clicks and out-of-area enquiries.
Structural Issue
Too Many Primary Conversion Goals Confusing the Algorithm
Multiple conversion actions have been added as primary goals. Google's algorithm tries to optimise for all of them simultaneously, spreading focus across high and low-value actions alike. This causes inefficient budget spending and poor bid decisions. Best practice is to designate only your most valuable bottom-funnel actions — specifically Valuation Form Submissions and Calls from Ads — as primary goals. All other conversion actions should be set to "secondary" status so they are tracked but do not influence bidding.
Budget / Bidding Mismatch
Let With Us Campaign: Daily Budget vs Target CPA Misalignment
The "Let With Us" campaign has a Target CPA of £27.00 but a daily budget of only £10.14. Google's algorithm requires sufficient data to learn and optimise — it needs to generate enough conversions for its machine learning to function. With a budget lower than your Target CPA, the campaign cannot afford a single full conversion per day, which prevents the algorithm from ever entering its efficient learning phase. The daily budget should be set to at least 2–3× the Target CPA, meaning a minimum of £54–£81/day is required for this campaign to function as designed.

04
Our Recommendations
1
Immediate fix — 1 day
Disable the Display Network from All Search Campaigns
Uncheck "Display Network" in both campaign settings. This single change immediately stops budget bleeding into irrelevant placements, restores accurate CTR and CPA data, and ensures every pound is spent on people actively searching on Google — not passively browsing the web.
2
Immediate fix — 1 day
Switch Location Targeting to "Presence Only"
Change your location targeting setting from "Presence or Interest" to "Presence Only" in both campaigns. This directly resolves your out-of-area lead problem by ensuring your ads are shown exclusively to users who are physically located in Tunbridge Wells and the surrounding Kent and Sussex areas you service.
3
Structural — 1 week
Set Only One Primary Conversion Goal per Campaign
For the Sell With Us campaign, set "Valuation Form Submit" as the sole primary goal. For Let With Us, set "Call from Ads" as primary. All other tracked actions move to secondary status. This gives Google's algorithm a clear, singular signal to optimise toward — dramatically improving bid efficiency and lead quality.
4
Structural — 1 week
Build a Negative Keyword List & Prioritise High-Intent Terms
Add an extensive negative keyword list (free, instant, calculator, checker, estimate, how much, what is, online valuation tool) to block informational traffic burning budget with no conversion intent. Simultaneously, increase match type control around "estate agents near me" and similar high-intent local terms — the only search query generating conversions in the Sell With Us campaign at a CPA of just £7.58.
5
Campaign revamp — 2–3 weeks
Restructure Ad Groups, Rewrite Ad Copy & Add Sitelinks
Reorganise keywords into tightly themed ad groups by search intent (e.g. Selling, Valuation, Local Agency searches). Rewrite ad copy to include target keywords in headlines, incorporate Kings Estates' USPs (independent, personalised service, Tunbridge Wells experts), and add strong calls-to-action. Add sitelink extensions immediately — they increase ad visibility, provide additional conversion pathways, and typically boost CTR by 10–20%.
6
Budget — immediate
Align the Let With Us Budget with the Target CPA
Increase the Let With Us daily budget to a minimum of £54/day (2× the £27 Target CPA). The current £10.14/day budget prevents Google's algorithm from ever gathering enough conversion data to optimise. This campaign already shows strong structural potential — a 6.56% CTR and 21 conversions — and will perform significantly better once the algorithm has the budget it needs to learn.
